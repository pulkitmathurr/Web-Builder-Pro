## Overview

Milestone 2 delivered **12 fully functional content modules**. Each module gives the school's
admin a dedicated management screen to add, edit, and publish content — and a matching page on
the public school website where parents, students, and visitors see that content presented in a
polished, modern design. Every module supports the school's chosen color theme, so the whole
website stays visually consistent no matter which pages a visitor browses.

Across every module, admins have full control over **what's visible to the public** — content
can be saved as a draft and published only when ready, so nothing goes live before the school
is ready to show it.

---

## 1. Home Page

The main landing page of the school's website — the first impression for every visitor.

- Full-screen hero banner introducing the school
- "About the School" snippet with a short introduction
- Key stats section (e.g. years of excellence, number of students, faculty, achievements)
- Highlights section showcasing what makes the school stand out

---

## 2. About Us

A complete "get to know the school" page, built from several sections the admin can fill in:

- **Vision & Mission cards** — a scrolling row of cards covering the school's guiding
  principles (vision, mission, motto, etc.)
- **Our History** — the school's founding story and journey, alongside a photo, with a
  "Read More" option for longer stories
- **Leadership Messages** — a message from the Principal, Chairman, or other leadership, each
  with their photo and designation
- **Core Values** — a numbered list of the values the school stands for
- **Photo gallery** — supporting images for the About page

---

## 3. Fee Structure

A clear, class-wise breakdown of school fees.

- Admin can build a dynamic table covering any number of classes and fee heads (tuition,
  transport, admission, etc.)
- Displayed publicly as a clean, easy-to-read fee table so parents can quickly check costs for
  their child's class

---

## 4. Courses & Streams

Academic information organized across the school's four levels — **Primary, Middle, High, and
Senior School** — each with its own dedicated page.

- **Overview page** — a summary card for each level with class range, description, and
  subjects offered
- Per-level detail page includes:
  - **About section** — a featured quote/description with photo, introducing that level
  - **"Why We're Unique" section** — what sets this level of schooling apart
  - **Photo gallery** — campus life images specific to that level
  - **Staff/contact cards** — key contacts for that level

---

## 5. Faculty

A showcase of the school's teaching staff, organized by school level (Primary/Middle/High/
Senior/General).

- Each teacher profile includes photo, name, designation, qualification, experience, and a
  short bio
- Visitors browse teachers through an interactive carousel of profile cards per level
- Rotating banner images at the top of the page

---

## 6. Infrastructure

A flexible showcase of the school's facilities — labs, library, playground, auditorium, or
anything else the school wants to highlight.

- Admin can create any number of custom categories, each with its own photos and description
- Public visitors browse categories through an easy tab/sidebar switcher, with an image slider
  for each category

---

## 7. Sports

A dedicated section covering the school's sporting life, split into four pages:

- **Sports at School** — a photo showcase of sporting life on campus
- **Sports Offered** — a list of every sport the school offers, each with photos and a
  description
- **Sporting Events** — write-ups of sporting events held at the school, with photos
- **Sports Awards & Achievements** — certifications earned, a "Making Us Proud" section
  highlighting student sporting achievements, and a year-wise table of downloadable
  award-winner PDFs

---

## 8. Gallery

A general-purpose photo and video gallery for the school.

- Supports **nested folders/albums** for both photos and videos, so content can be organized
  by event, year, or category
- Photos open in a full-screen lightbox viewer
- Videos can be uploaded directly or added via YouTube links

---

## 9. Achievements

A page celebrating the school's and students' achievements and recognitions.

- Each achievement entry includes a photo, title, category, year, and write-up
- A **Certifications** section displays earned certificates as cards, which open in a detailed
  modal view (with zoom) when clicked

---

## 10. Alumni

A page celebrating the school's alumni and their accomplishments after graduating.

- Each alumni entry includes photo, name, graduating batch year, a headline achievement (e.g.
  "Currently at SRCC, cleared CA Foundation"), a testimonial, and an optional LinkedIn profile
  link
- Displayed in an elegant, magazine-style list with a polaroid-photo aesthetic

---

## 11. Public Disclosure

A compliance/transparency page (as commonly required by education boards) presenting official
school information and documents.

- Admin can organize information into flexible categories — either plain informational text,
  or downloadable documents (PDF upload, external link, or description)
- Comes pre-set with standard categories (General Information, Documents and Information,
  Result and Academics) and supports adding custom categories

---

## 12. Transfer Certificate (TC) Information

A self-service tool letting parents and former students look up and download their Transfer
Certificate.

- Records are organized by academic session (e.g. "2023-24"), each holding TC number, student
  name, class, and a link to the certificate PDF
- Admin can bulk-upload records via a spreadsheet/CSV, or add them individually
- On the public site, visitors select their session, then search by TC number and student name
  to instantly find and download their certificate

---

## Net Result

With these 12 modules, every school on the platform gets a complete, professional website
covering academics, faculty, facilities, sports, achievements, alumni, compliance documents,
and self-service TC lookup — all manageable by the school's own admin team without needing any
technical help, and all styled consistently to match the school's chosen theme.
